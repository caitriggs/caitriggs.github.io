Option B · Southern basecamp
The Ancient Lakes Winery Circuit

Drive-up coulee-lake camping in the Quincy Wildlife Area, built around the tightest winery cluster in the region — Cave B, Beaumont, and Jones all within ~10 miles of camp.

Pick this if: the winery day is the point, and you want intimate coulee-lake camping close to the vineyards.

01
FRI · JUL 31
Claim a coulee-lake site
≈ 3:00 PM · Arrive
Quincy Lakes / Burke Lake — Basecamp (Nights 1 & 2)
47.15501, -119.91789

Drive-up dispersed on WDFW land — the gate is open Mar 1–Sep 30, so you drive right in to lakeside or bench sites with sunset views. Vault toilets throughout, free with Discover Pass, well-spaced. Leave the west side ~11 AM (≈3 hr) to claim a good spot on a summer Friday. Note: leeches reported in the lake (paddling fine, swimming iffy), plus mosquitoes — bring spray.

Alt basecamp
Frenchman Coulee (dramatic overlook)
47.02785, -119.96708

Swap here for the best sunset of any option: basalt columns, a waterfall across the canyon, and an "Overland Overlook" site made for rigs like ours. Trade-off: busier with climbers on weekends, windy, rocky ground, steep entrance (4×4 clearance helps). Off I-90 Exit 143.

SAT · AUG 1
The winery circuit
12:00 PM · Marquee
Cave B Estate Winery
47.09667, -119.99544

Start on the cliff, 900 ft above the Columbia. $20 for 6 wines / $25 keep-the-glass. Open Fri–Sat 11–6:30, last tasting 45 min before close. Dogs OK leashed & outside per county rule. Reserve online or walk in.

2:00 PM · ~8 mi
Beaumont Cellars
47.20934, -119.95055

Relaxed farm setting with covered patio seating. Well-reviewed Tempranillo, friendly pours, resident chocolate lab. Fri–Sat 12–6, Sun 12–5. No reservation needed.

3:30 PM · In town
Jones of Washington
47.23262, -119.86954

Close it out casual in Quincy town: Ancient Lakes whites — Riesling, Pinot Gris, Chardonnay — plus the wine slushies reviewers swear by on a 95°F day. Sat 12–6. Then back to camp for sunset.

Optional add-ons
White Heron · Chris Daniel

Two more nearby for a fuller circuit — White Heron (European-style, cliff views above Crescent Bar) and Chris Daniel in George (Fri–Sun 12–5). Easy to swap in for Beaumont or Jones.

03
SUN · AUG 2
Coulee hike & drive home
≈ 9:00 AM · Before heat
Ancient Lakes / Dusty Lake day hike
47.16031, -119.98070

Morning hike into the waterfall-fed coulee — flat, scenic, and camp gear stays at the rig. Go early before the sun bakes the exposed basin, then break camp. Don't drink or filter the lake water — it's ag runoff.

≈ 12:00 PM · On the way
Frenchman Coulee (optional detour)
47.02785, -119.96708

A quick scenic stop on the way to I-90 if you didn't camp here: basalt columns, the waterfall, and a Columbia River swim at the end of Vantage Rd to rinse off before the drive home.

Water fill-up · Southern route

No potable water at camp, so tank up before or en route. Closest reliable fills: the town of Quincy (gas stations & grocery, ~15 min from camp) on the way in, and Potholes State Park to the south, which has potable water, showers, and a dump station if you want a full top-off.

Option A · Northern basecamp
Big-Water Camp & the Dam Show

Drive-up sites right on Banks Lake, a 27-mile reservoir you can actually swim in, with the marquee Ice Age Floods geology on your doorstep and a free laser show on Grand Coulee Dam for Max's birthday eve.

Pick this if: swimming, big-water camping, and a birthday spectacle matter most — wine becomes a single stop on the way home.

Osborn Bay — Basecamp (Nights 1 & 2)
47.92279, -119.06037

Drive-up primitive sites right on Banks Lake off Hwy 155, WDFW/State Parks. ~$12/night, vault toilet, boat ramp & dock, first-come. No water/power. ~3.5 hr from the west side. Ticks reported — nightly checks including the dog. Through-traffic on the camp road at night per reviews.

Fallback
Jones Bay (if Osborn's full)
≈ 47.9445, -119.0866

Same primitive drive-up setup a couple miles up Hwy 155 — part of the same 70+ site stretch on Banks Lake. Good backup on a summer Friday.

02
SAT · AUG 1
Swim, then read the flood
Dawn (optional) · summit
Steamboat Rock Trail
47.86080, -119.12360

3.2 mi round trip, ~760 ft gain with light scrambling, onto a 600-acre basalt plateau above the lake. No shade, rattlesnakes, can top 100°F — dawn only, or skip on the hottest day.

≈ 10:00 AM · Cool off
Steamboat Rock swim beach
47.86080, -119.12360

Sandy swim beach on Banks Lake's blue water, ~7 mi south of camp. Morning swim before the desert bakes. Discover Pass for day use.

≈ 1:00 PM · Geology
Dry Falls Visitor Center
47.60650, -119.36469

The 400-ft cliff that was once the largest waterfall on Earth — a 3.5-mi-wide dead cataract. AC, clean restrooms, and the interpretive story that frames the whole landscape. ~30 min south.

≈ 2:30 PM · Turnaround
Lake Lenore Caves
47.51731, -119.49372

Short, rocky hike to basalt shelter caves used by early peoples — high reward, low effort. Watch footing on loose rock. Southern turnaround; head back north to camp.

≈ 9:30 PM · Birthday eve
Grand Coulee Dam laser show
47.96043, -118.98667

Free laser light show projected on the dam face, nightly through September. ~15 min from camp — a memorable low-key send-off into Max's birthday week. Confirm tonight's start time at the visitor center earlier in the day (it shifts with sunset).

Water fill-up · Northern route

Sun Lakes-Dry Falls State Park has potable water, showers, and a dump station — it's right on the Saturday geology route, so top off there. Even closer, the developed Steamboat Rock State Park campground (~7 mi south of camp) has drinking water and showers by the day-use area.
