Option A: Print and mail

Every letter prints with your name and address filled in: one page each, with its sources on the back, plus a page of envelope addresses.

Print double-sided (flip on long edge) so each letter's sources land on the back. Sign each one and mail it with 1 Forever stamp per envelope.

Option B: Email

Members of Congress don't publish email addresses. Their offices take messages through a contact form on each member's website, and many forms only accept people who live in that state or district.

Use the buttons next to each name under Your letters to:

  • Copy letter and paste it into the member's contact form.
  • Gmail, Outlook or Email app to open a draft with the letter filled in. Send it to a friend who lives in that member's state or district so they can send it in, or to yourself to print later.
